Hiking in Australia

Exploring hiking participation across Australia and how people experience NSW national parks.

Note: AusPlay reports hiking-related participation under the activity category “Bush walking”.

1. Hiking participation in Australia

Hiking is a popular outdoor activity across Australia. AusPlay records this activity as “Bush walking”, allowing us to compare its popularity, age profile and recent participation trends.

Source: Australian Sports Commission, AusPlay , 30 April 2026.

2. Where is hiking most popular?

Hiking participation varies across Australia. Tasmania and the ACT have the highest participation rates, while NSW has the largest estimated number of people taking part.

Source: Australian Sports Commission, AusPlay state and territory data tables .

3. Hiking in the context of NSW national parks

To place hiking participation in a broader outdoor context, we look at how NSW national park visitation varies across regions and over time. Greater Sydney records the highest recent visitation.

Greater Sydney records the highest estimated park visitation, with the gap widening noticeably in FY24–25.

Source: NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, Parks Visitation Survey FY24–25 .

4. What do NSW national park visits look like?

Most walking trips in NSW national parks last between one and four hours, while interstate visitors come most strongly from Queensland and Victoria.

Source: NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, Parks Visitation Survey FY24–25 .
