Walking Australia

Exploring who bushwalks, where participation is strongest, and how Australians engage with NSW national parks.

1. Bushwalking in Australia

Bushwalking is one of Australia’s most popular physical activities. Participation varies across age groups, and much of it happens outside formally organised activities.

Source: Australian Sports Commission, AusPlay, 30 April 2026.

2. Where are Australia’s bushwalkers?

Participation rate and participant numbers tell different stories. Smaller states can have very high participation rates, while larger states contain more bushwalkers overall.

Source: Australian Sports Commission, AusPlay state and territory data tables.

3. From bushwalking to NSW national parks

Looking more closely at NSW shows how visitation differs between park regions and how strongly Greater Sydney contributes to overall park use.

Source: NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, Parks Visitation Survey FY24–25.

4. What does a park visit look like?

Most park walks last between one and four hours, while interstate visitation is concentrated most strongly from Queensland and Victoria.

Source: NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service, Parks Visitation Survey FY24–25.
