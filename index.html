Hiking in Australia

Exploring hiking participation across Australia and experiences within New South Wales national parks.

AusPlay reports hiking under “Bush walking”, and this terminology is retained throughout.

1. Hiking participation in Australia

Hiking is popular across Australia. AusPlay records this activity as “Bush walking”, allowing comparisons across popularity, age and recent participation trends.

Key takeaway: Bush walking ranks third among major activities, organised participation remains much lower than total participation, and the overall rate increased slightly from 2024 to 2025.

2. Where is hiking most popular?

Hiking participation varies across Australia. Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory record the highest participation rates, while New South Wales has the largest estimated participant population.

04

Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory lead hiking participation

Share of adults participating in “Bush walking”, 2025

05

New South Wales has Australia's largest estimated hiking population

Estimated adults participating in “Bush walking”, 2025

Key takeaway: Tasmania and the Australian Capital Territory have the highest participation rates, while New South Wales has the largest estimated hiking population. A high participation rate does not necessarily mean the largest number of participants.

3. Where can people hike in New South Wales?

Participation shows where hiking is popular, but not where people can hike. Tracks managed by the New South Wales National Parks and Wildlife Service (NPWS) indicate potential formal hiking opportunities.

4. Hiking in the context of New South Wales national parks

Many hiking opportunities sit within New South Wales national parks. Park visitation provides context for how regional use has changed and how long walking visits can last. These figures also include activities beyond hiking.

Key takeaway: Greater Sydney records the highest recent park visitation, with its lead increasing strongly in FY24–25. One-to-four-hour walks are the most common reported duration.
